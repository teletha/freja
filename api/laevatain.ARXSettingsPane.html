A Windows 11-style settings surface: an ARXNavList rail on the left and one continuous, scrollable document on the right. Every section registers a rail entry; choosing an entry glides the viewport to that section, and scrolling keeps the rail selection in sync.

The building blocks the pages are made of - rounded cards of label-and-control rows separated by hairlines - are exposed as statics so any host can compose its own pages. Colours and fonts are read from the ARX.* UI defaults, keeping the component independent of a concrete theme class.
