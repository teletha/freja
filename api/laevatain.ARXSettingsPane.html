A Windows 11-style settings surface: an ARXNavList rail on the left and one continuous, scrollable document on the right. Every section registers a rail entry; choosing an entry glides the viewport to that section, scrolling keeps the rail in sync, and the search field in its own pane above the document narrows the whole page to the rows which answer to the query.

A page is built from rows inside rounded cards, and rows carry the words they answer to: their caption, their description, the English origin of both and whatever extra terms the host registers. That way "フォント", "font" and "typeface" all find the same setting, whatever language the page is currently shown in.

search(Sentenceplaceholder, SentencenoneMessage)void

Sentenceplaceholder
SentencenoneMessage

Installs the search field in its own pane above the document. Typing narrows every page to the rows which answer to the query, hides the pages and rail entries left empty, and glides to the first hit; clearing the field restores the whole document. Both captions follow their variables, so the field reads in the current language, and Ctrl+F reaches it from anywhere inside the page.
